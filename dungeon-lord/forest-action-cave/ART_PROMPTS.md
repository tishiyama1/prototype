# New cave layer art — final prompt set

Mode: built-in imagegen (not CLI). Four distinct asset calls; genuine alpha requested for far/middle/foreground. Input `IMG_8744(1).jpeg` was a mood/palette/material reference, not an edit target. The floor and subsequent generated layers were added as style references to later calls. The user's reference image is not republished in this repo.

Delivery locations: `forest-action-cave/media/ground.webp`, `far.webp`, `middle.webp`, `foreground.webp`. Original outputs were PNG; WebP conversion and transparent-margin trimming only pack the images and preserve their alpha/geometry anchors. Scene dimensions and offsets are in `scene.json`. No manual repainting, forest-background bake or new 3D models were used.

## Ground

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a game ground texture, one layer of a layered 2D cave environment for Forest Action. Input image is ONLY a reference for palette, stone texture and warm lantern illumination, NOT a layout to copy. Create a NEW detailed painted cave floor viewed from a STRICT VERTICAL ORTHOGRAPHIC TOP-DOWN camera, no perspective or horizon. Wide 4:3 landscape, preferably 2048x1536. Covers a broad underground chamber of dusty stone and compacted brown-grey earth; winding worn paving and shallow stone slabs lead from lower centre toward upper centre. Blue charcoal-grey edges, desaturated stone grey-brown centre; gentle amber light pools in upper-left quarter and a smaller upper-right pool as if lanterns stood just outside the image. Central 60% must be open flat walkable ground with medium values and quiet textures so small pixel characters stay legible. Tiny scattered pebbles, worn cracks, subtle moisture, natural irregular patches, detailed handpainted stylized miniature quality. Do not put tall walls, columns, timber props, lamps, large boulders or people in this FLOOR asset. Surface details only, no baked character shadows, no black void, no dramatic bevel island shape, no frame, no UI, no text. It must fill every pixel and blend quietly at outer edges. This is a true flat floor MATERIAL image to place on an XZ plane, not a complete cave illustration.

## Far

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: FAR BACKGROUND transparent environment layer for a 2.5D tactical cave game. Reference image 1 supplies the quiet charcoal-blue cave and amber-lantern mood. Reference image 2 is the NEW game floor whose stone texture and painted style must match. Create new art, not a copy. Wide landscape 3:2. Paint a receding ancient cave passage viewed from an elevated approximately 43-degree orthographic/isometric front camera. A low broad irregular stone arch opens near the lower-centre into a long, very dark winding passage; nested shadowy rock arches and a faint tiny amber lamp deep inside suggest depth. Blue-grey stratified rock overhead and at either side, desaturated atmospheric shadow, detailed sculptural hand-painted miniature style rather than coarse polygons. The opening must be clearly readable in the LOWER HALF of the image, not high near the top. The centre is quiet and dark but the nearest passage floor is readable. This cutout represents the distant space BEHIND a wide combat chamber. No near foreground boulders, no player, no enemy, no UI or lettering. Outside the isolated passage/rock arch is genuinely TRANSPARENT ALPHA, not a black rectangular background or checkerboard drawn into the art. Interior darkness of the passage is real opaque dark rock atmosphere, not transparent. Feathered irregular boundary around the distant arch, and a narrow tapered strip of dim floor at bottom so this can overlap a separate cave ground texture naturally. No large main lantern in this far layer; that belongs in a separate midground asset.

## Middle

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent MIDGROUND environment sprite for a layered cave combat scene. Use the provided original cave only for mood; match the new generated floor and distant tunnel art in the other references. NEW art. Landscape 3:2, high quality detailed stylized painted miniature. Two SEPARATE side clusters on the same transparent canvas: an irregular slate-blue stratified ROCK WALL at the far LEFT, with old splintered timber support and one hanging brass oil LANTERN glowing amber near the lower inner edge; and a narrower jagged ROCK PILLAR / wall fragment at the far RIGHT with chipped stone, small rubble at its base and a much dimmer warm reflection. Walls can have broad geological facets but avoid coarse low-poly triangles and toy geometry. The lantern must look aged and practical, with a cage, soot, glass and a suspension rope; glow soft, not huge bloom. Elevated 43-degree orthographic/isometric front view appropriate for a top-down tactical arena. The centre SIXTY PERCENT of the canvas is completely EMPTY transparent alpha, an unobstructed combat opening. No connecting arch across the centre, no wall or floor behind the gap. Left and right clusters stand along the lateral margins, rooted at the lower edge, with irregular broken silhouettes, climbing toward the top. The lamp sits in the lower third so it remains visible with the upper wall cropped by the game viewport. Transparent exterior and transparent space between the clusters, no opaque rectangle or checkerboard. No people, monsters, lettering, UI, tree roots, leaves or forest vegetation. Add subtle material wear and moisture, old mining/exploration traces; preserve the reference's charcoal-blue versus amber contrast.

## Foreground

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: FOREGROUND transparent frame layer for a 2.5D cave battle game. Match the material detail and charcoal-blue/amber lighting of the reference cave and generated midground. Create new art. Very wide landscape 2:1. Two separate low rocky shelves/wedges at the far LEFT and far RIGHT corners, dark blue-grey weathered stone with irregular rounded chips, thin sediment cracks and small rubble. Seen from an elevated 43-degree orthographic front view. One small broken stalagmite at a corner is acceptable, but keep props low and readable, no giant vertical pillars. Warm reflected amber light brushes inward-facing rock edges; exterior faces remain quiet dark slate. This is a gentle framing layer, not the whole cave. At least the middle seventy percent is entirely EMPTY transparent alpha, with no object or floor spanning the central area. Irregular authentic stone silhouettes, painterly miniature game art, avoid coarse polygon-toy surfaces. Low rocks occupy only the outermost corners and lower band so they will not cover combat characters. Transparent background, actual alpha everywhere outside the isolated rock wedges and specks; no opaque rectangle, no checkerboard, no letters, no characters, no lanterns, no vegetation. Do not paint a complete cave scene or background behind the rocks.

Different viewpoints would need separately planned art. The intended transparent central gap is checked using actual alpha, not inferred from the appearance of the generator preview.
